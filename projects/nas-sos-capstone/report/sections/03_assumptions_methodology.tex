\subsection{The Two Claims}
\label{sec:two-claims}

\plantodo{Track C (D-016, D-017)}{\textbf{Separate the two claims and say
which the numbers support.} Claim 1, tested by the experiment: replacing the
decider's low-fidelity rule of thumb with a higher-fidelity model fed by
weight and cost index across the airline-to-ATC interface changes which
aircraft is moved and lowers total system cost (whether one extra clearance
is worth it is a result, not an assumption). Claim 2, shown by the model:
the architecture locates that interface, names who owns the decision and the
data, states the unmet requirement, and lets the scoring model's fidelity be
swapped (conceptual, nominal mass, actual mass) with the structure
unchanged. The simulation would give the same numbers without the model;
say so, and say what the model contributes that the numbers do not.}
