Wording in quotes is the handwriting; anything in square brackets is my guess at an unclear word. Page numbers are the numbers printed at the foot of each page. Section numbers are the report's as printed, before the restructure these notes caused. "ACM" in the notes is the owner's term, aircraft capability model. "Kind" is my reading of the note:

- **change**: the note says what the report should say or contain.
- **strike**: text crossed out or scribbled over, with no words.
- **move**: the note sends content to another place.
- **question**: the note asks; the last column says what was done with it.
- **check**: the note asks for something to be verified.
- **do**: the note marks a placeholder as work to be done now.
