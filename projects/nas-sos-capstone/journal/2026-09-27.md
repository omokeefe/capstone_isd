TMUs at ARTCCs and designated terminal facilities (¶18-2-4a1) send operational data to the Air Traffic Control System Command Center (ATCSCC), which is "delegated the authority to direct the operation of the TM system" (¶18-2-3). The purpose is to balance NAS capacity with demand (¶18-1-1). The exchange happens through the National Traffic Management Log (NTML), which facilities must use "in preference to other methods" (¶18-5-3, ¶18-5-8b). It also happens through TFMS and through telcons and hotlines (¶18-4-1). Verbal coordination is required whenever electronic coordination isn't complete or isn't appropriate (¶18-5-1, ¶18-5-10c). Terminal facilities normally go **through the ARTCC TMU**, which coordinates with the ATCSCC on their behalf (¶18-2-4c NOTE, ¶18-5-4d). A terminal TMU may still call the ATCSCC directly in unusual circumstances (¶18-5-7b).

Main categories of TMU → ATCSCC data:

1. **Constraints and system adverse impacts.** The facility notifies the ATCSCC "as soon as the facility becomes aware" of any component change that may affect NAS capacity. This is done by telephone or hotline and entered in the NTML (¶18-2-4a6 + NOTE; ¶18-5-4c1, c3; ¶18-5-4g4).
   - Infrastructure outages: runway closures, route/airway closures, ILS/equipment outages, computer malfunctions or outages, TELCO outages (¶18-2-4a6). **Planned** outages and events go in advance as an electronic System Impact Report (SIR). The TMU develops it, the DDSO/OSG enters it on the SEC site, and the ATCSCC approves it before customers can see it (¶18-5-13). Technical Operations reports unplanned FAA equipment outages.
   - Critical facility changes: NAVAID/radar shutdowns, procedural changes affecting key terminals and/or centers (¶18-2-4a6). Also staffing, hurricane preparedness, forest fires (¶18-5-4c1).
   - Weather constraints: facility TMUs "must coordinate with the ATCSCC for matters pertaining to severe weather." The ATCSCC is the final approving authority for alternate routes that cross facility boundaries (¶18-15-2). SWAP exists as §18-16 (not read in detail).
2. **Traffic Management Initiatives (TMIs).** The ATCSCC approves all interfacility TMIs, except MIT of 10 miles or less. TMIs expected to cause reportable delays (≥ 15 min, per JO 7210.55) must be coordinated through the ATCSCC (¶18-7-7a). TMIs inside an ARTCC's own jurisdiction that don't cause reportable delays stay with the Center/TRACON (¶18-7-7b, ¶18-7-11). Every TMI request carries the constraint, the internal actions already taken, the help needed, and the system impacts (¶18-7-9c). TMIs are documented in the NTML (¶18-7-9d).
   - Ground Delay Programs (GDPs): **Correction.** The TMU does not coordinate EDCTs; the **ATCSCC issues them** "to ARTCCs and linked system users" (¶18-10-6e). The ARTCC TMU assigns EDCTs from the DAS table only for aircraft that didn't receive one. It keeps the ATCSCC apprised of cancellations and diversions, relays terminal EDCT issues, and requests revised EDCTs from the ATCSCC (¶18-10-7). Terminals send hourly arrival/departure counts to the ATCSCC while a GDP is in effect (¶18-10-8h).
   - Ground Stops (GS): confirmed. A local GS (conditions ≤ 30 min, wholly inside the facility's area) requires notifying the ATCSCC **before** implementing it, or as soon as practical if conditions prohibit that. It can't be extended without ATCSCC approval (¶18-13-3).
   - In-trail restrictions: **Correction to scope.** Sector-to-sector MIT *inside* a facility is local latitude and not an ATCSCC matter (¶18-2-3 NOTE). *Interfacility* restrictions are entered in the NTML and accepted by providing facilities within 15 minutes. The ATCSCC approves or denies the ones that need its review (¶18-5-11). MIT of 10 or less is coordinated facility to facility, lasts at most 4 h, and goes to the ATCSCC only on disagreement (¶18-7-12). MIT of 25 or more requires a shared FEA (¶18-7-13a). MIT of 40 or more requires a conference with the ATCSCC (¶18-7-13d).
   - Time-Based Management (TBM/TBFM): confirmed. The TMU reports the status of TBM operations (¶18-5-4c1) and coordinates interfacility TBM with the ATCSCC, which is the approval authority (¶18-6-8, ¶18-6-9b, ¶18-6-11b).
3. **Airspace structure and flow-modeling data.**
   - Flow Evaluation Areas (FEAs) and Flow Constrained Areas (FCAs): confirmed. TMUs share FEAs that may require TMIs (reroutes, MIT, ground stops) with the ATCSCC. They contact the ATCSCC to coordinate a public FEA or an FCA (¶18-8-4a3–4). ARTCC-to-ARTCC FEA/FCA coordination is in ¶18-8-5.
   - Reroute requests: **Correction.** A TMU requesting a reroute notifies the ATCSCC **via the NTML** of the FEA and the proposed reroute (¶18-8-4a3). **Trajectory Options Sets are not TMU data.** A TOS is "a message sent by participating flight operators to TFMS" (¶18-7-4h6, ¶18-12-3b), so it belongs on the AOC ↔ TFM link. **Route Amendment Dialog (RAD)** is the ARTCC TMC's TFMS tool for defining airborne reroutes, which it sends **to ERAM** for the sector controllers to execute (¶18-7-4h5). That is a TMU → controller flow, not TMU → ATCSCC.
4. **Metrics, delays, and status data.**
   - Delay reports: confirmed, with thresholds. Verbally notify the ATCSCC of any delay reaching or expected to reach 15 min, then keep updating status in the NTML. Notify the overlying ARTCC TMO at 45 min. Verbally notify the ATCSCC at 90 min and document it. EDCT delays from GDP/AFP/CTOP/GS issued by the ATCSCC are exempt (¶18-5-12).
   - Monitor Alert Parameter (MAP) alerts: **Correction.** Red and yellow alerts are **not sent to the ATCSCC**. The TMU monitors them in TFMS, analyzes them, and notifies the affected *area* inside the facility. It keeps a 45-day log of red alerts (¶18-9-3d–e). What goes to the ATCSCC is any **adjustment of MAP values** (¶18-9-2b). The 1.5–2.5 h look-ahead is confirmed as the recommended setting, with action about 1 h before the alerted period (¶18-9-3c).
   - Agreements and directives: confirmed. Facilities provide the ATCSCC with "all formal agreements and directives that relate to interfacility TM programs, initiatives, and procedures" (¶18-2-4a3).

**Flows the note missed (TMU/facility → ATCSCC, JO 7210.3EE):**
- Runway configurations with their AAR/ADR, entered in the NTML by ASPM airports (¶18-5-4e1). Hourly arrival/departure counts go on the ATCSCC web page (¶18-5-4e2).
- Static airport data (AARs by weather category, program rates, holding capacities, TM Tips), sent on a fixed schedule through the DDSO (¶18-5-5).
- Deicing status (in/out) and equipment-caused TMIs marked "(E)" in the NTML (¶18-5-4g).
- Unresolved conflicts between adjacent TMUs, sent to the ATCSCC for resolution (¶18-5-4c2, ¶18-7-12d).
- Hotline requests, including which facilities and customers should take part (¶18-5-4c5). Start or end of extended flight-plan drop times (¶18-5-4c6).
- Operations Plan input from the District TMO: outages, internal initiatives, terminal constraints, route closures, anticipated TBM/TMIs (¶18-21-3d).
