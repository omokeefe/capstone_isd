Built and shipped PDF-annotation extraction tooling: `tools/extract_pdf_annotations.py`
(PyMuPDF-based) pulls highlights, underlines, strikeouts, and typed sticky-note/comment
text out of an annotated PDF's text layer, with the exact quoted passage under each
markup. Handwritten (ink) annotations are opt-in only (`--include-handwritten`) — ink

strokes for one 5-page PDF), so the tool clusters nearby strokes into whole notes before
rendering an image crop per note, rather than one illegible fragment per stroke. Verified
against the three sources that actually have digital markup
