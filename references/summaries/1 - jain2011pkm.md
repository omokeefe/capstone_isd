## What it is

An exploratory, survey-based library/information-science journal article (University of
Botswana). Reviews PKM literature (definitions, skills, tools, adoption stages) and
reports a small questionnaire study (N=54 faculty respondents) on awareness of Personal
Knowledge Management (PKM) and its perceived link to individual/organizational
productivity. Concludes PKM should be a foundation of organizational knowledge management
and recommends training/incentive/goal-alignment programs.
