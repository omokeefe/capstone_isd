## What it is

A software-assisted Systematic Literature Review (SWARM-SLR workflow, 164 of 1,000+
candidate papers analyzed) cataloguing knowledge-based engineering (KBE) and
knowledge-management practices in aerospace engineering. Produces a taxonomy/knowledge
graph of 700+ processes, tools, and data formats (OWL-formalized, linked to
Wikidata/Zenodo/ORKG) organized around how aerospace engineers externalize, utilize, and
exchange explicit knowledge. Methodology/tooling-and-practices review (KBE, ontologies,
digital thread, PLM, V-model), not an operations or optimization paper.

## Why it's valuable — and to what

- Literature review section: none of §2-4 — design-phase knowledge engineering, not
  day-of-ops airline/ATC operations.
- Decomposition / architecture (§6, §10): main relevance point — surveys MBSE-adjacent
  practices (V-model, digital thread, ontology-based knowledge capture) analogous to the
  capstone's own approach of tracing intent through a SysML/Cameo SoS model; usable as
  methodology background/justification for structured, ontology-backed knowledge
  representation.
- Stakeholder / objective ontology (§7-9): weak/indirect — about knowledge-representation
  tooling, not decision ontologies.
- Optimization study (§11-13): not relevant.
- Glossary / terminology: Knowledge-Based Engineering (KBE), FAIR principles, digital
  thread, V-model, SWARM-SLR, knowledge graph, Semantic Web (RDF/OWL/SPARQL).

## Rating

**2/5** — Well-executed SLR but one level removed from the capstone's storyline (NAS SoS,
intent propagation, airline ops). Value is as generic methodology background for
ontology-based knowledge representation in complex aerospace engineering — supportive if
§6/§10 wants a citation for MBSE/knowledge-engineering rigor, but doesn't inform
operational/stakeholder/optimization content.
