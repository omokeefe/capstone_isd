## What it is

A review of aircraft ground operations centered on the turnaround: current challenges
(airport capacity, schedule disruptions, cost pressure), boarding-strategy and cabin-layout
research aimed at shortening turnaround time, and a summary of existing modeling/simulation
frameworks (agent-based, discrete-event, cellular automaton) used to assess turnaround
concepts.
