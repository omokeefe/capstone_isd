Follow `assistant/workflows/session-tagup.md` exactly: a quick silent context refresh,
then a compact `persona-pm` status report (overall verdict first, what's due/at-risk in
the next week, anything slipped, a recommended action), then a short `persona-professor`
note flagging the one substantive thing the schedule view wouldn't catch. Keep both
parts brief — this is a tag-up, not a full review. Append the finished tag-up to today's
`assistant/journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md` before finishing — this is a required step, not
optional bookkeeping.
