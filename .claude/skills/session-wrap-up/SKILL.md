Follow `assistant/workflows/session-wrap-up.md` exactly: write a session log to
`assistant/tasks/sessions/` from `assistant/templates/session-log-template.md`, update
`assistant/tasks/task-board.md`, sync any stale `assistant/memory/*.md` files
(project-brief, decisions-log, open-questions, source-register as applicable), and
confirm `Project_To-Do List.md` checkboxes reflect what was actually completed.
