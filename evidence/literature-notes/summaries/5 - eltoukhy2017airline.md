## What it is

A comprehensive literature review of airline schedule planning, covering the four
sequential subproblems — flight scheduling (FSP), fleet assignment (FAP), aircraft
maintenance routing (AMRP), and crew scheduling (CSP) — with a new classification of the
literature by model characteristics rather than solution methodology, plus future-research
directions (including integrated optimization).

## Why it's valuable — and to what
