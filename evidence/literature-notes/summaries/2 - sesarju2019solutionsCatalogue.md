## What it is

A catalogue of SESAR solutions, one or two pages each, grouped by key feature: high-performing airport operations, advanced air traffic services, optimised ATM network services, enabling aviation infrastructure, with delivered solutions first and candidate solutions after. About 150 pages. The introduction frames the SESAR vision, the European ATM Master Plan, the innovation pipeline and the performance ambition.

## Why it's valuable, and to what

- Literature review section: none of §2 to §4. Background for the architecture.
- Decomposition / architecture (§6, §10): the European counterpart to the FAA NAS infrastructure roadmaps (`faaNasInfrastructureRoadmaps2025`): a list of fielded and candidate capabilities, each with the operational need, the solution and its benefits. The place to cite the air-ground datalink and trajectory-based operations solutions (for example the ADS-C extended projected profile downlink of the FMS trajectory) that the report positions the proposed airline-to-ATC performance share against (D-019): the same kind of aircraft-derived data, downlinked to ATC, for a different decision.
- Optimization study (§11 to §13): none directly.

## Rating

**2/5.** A reference catalogue, useful for one or two citations in the research-gap and proposed-interface paragraphs; too broad and too European to carry evidence for the NAS model.

## Flags

- Registered from the title page, the imprint page and the table of contents only. Before citing a specific solution, read its page and note the page number here.
- `sesarju2025masterPlan` (rated 5) is the stronger SESAR source for direction; this catalogue is for naming specific solutions.
