## What it is

A primary MBSE methodology/roadmap study. Proposes a semantic MBSE research and practice roadmap
built on two core mechanisms: the KARMA (Kombination of ARchitecture Model specificAtion) modeling
language, and an Industrial Ontologies Foundry (IOF) systems-engineering/MBSE ontology, aiming to
solve model/data interoperability problems across MBSE tools and address the gap left by
graphical-only specifications like SysML.
