## What it is

A primary research-roadmap article from the Systems Engineering Research Center (SERC).
Distinguishes and structures two transformation threads: AI4SE (using AI/ML to augment and
automate systems engineering practice) and SE4AI (applying systems engineering methods to design
and operate learning-based/autonomous systems), organized around a "digital engineering as
enabler" framework spanning velocity, security, and AI-and-autonomy research verticals.
