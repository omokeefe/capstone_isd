## What it is

A primary modeling-theory/metamodeling comparative study from the University of Leipzig. Analyzes
and compares six meta-metamodels/metamodeling tools (ARIS, Ecore, GME, GOPPRR/MetaEdit+, MS DSL
Tools, MS Visio) against a common framework of first-class metamodeling concepts (object,
relationship, role, port, model, attribute types; inheritance; grouping; constraint languages),
presented as a comparison table and supporting relationship-pattern taxonomy.
