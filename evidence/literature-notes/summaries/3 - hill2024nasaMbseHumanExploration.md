## What it is

Describes NASA's Digital Engineering Transformation strategy — adopting MBSE/SysML to link
Headquarters, Programs, and field-center engineering teams for Moon-to-Mars human
exploration mission planning, framed around four transformation quadrants (Engineering,
Discovery, Decision-Making, Operations) and enabling technologies (AI/ML, model-based
anything, zero-trust architecture).

## Why it's valuable — and to what

- Literature review section: none / MBSE methodology background.
- Decomposition / architecture (§6, §10): a large-scale, multi-organization enterprise
  case study of applying MBSE across an agency-wide program — useful analog for arguing that
  MBSE is appropriate at NAS enterprise scale, not just component scale (contrast with the
  narrower `jagtap2025mbseEngineInlet` case study).
- Stakeholder / objective ontology (§7-§9): illustrates cross-organization
  (HQ/Program/field-center) information flow challenges structurally similar to
  cross-agency NAS coordination (FAA/DoD/ANSP/airlines), though the domain itself (space
  exploration) is unrelated.
- Glossary / terminology: Digital Engineering Transformation, Digital Thread (cross-reference
  with `lupp2026reactMbseMdo`, which covers Digital Thread in more depth).
