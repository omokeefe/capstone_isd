## What it is

A primary EU-China Horizon 2020 project deliverable (technical report). Performs a detailed,
comparative analysis of current Trajectory-Based Operations (TBO) principles and ATM baselines in
Europe and China, incorporates ICAO future-ATM-research requirements, conducts a flight-centered
analysis of the most fuel-efficient way of flying a best-case scenario, and derives a toolbox of
"greener ATM" concept elements used to construct a concrete greener-ATM concept for a TMA/medium
airport use case.

## Why it's valuable — and to what

- Literature review section: `to-do-list.md` §2-§4 (baseline ATM operations) and directly informs
  the ConOps framing (§5).
- Decomposition / architecture (§6, §10): TBO is precisely the operational paradigm this
  capstone's "trajectory-intent" propagation is built around — this document is a rigorous,
  authoritative, up-to-date (2020) baseline of what TBO concepts actually are in both European and
  Chinese practice, directly usable as grounding for how trajectory intent is defined, shared, and
  updated across the ATM system.
- Stakeholder / objective ontology (§7-§9): documents ATM stakeholder roles and requirements
  implicit in the TBO baseline comparison.
- Optimization study (§11-§13): the flight-centered fuel-efficiency best-case analysis is directly
  relevant grounding for optimization framing (what "efficient" trajectory execution looks like).
- Glossary / terminology: TBO, "greener ATM concept," and related EU/China ATM terminology.
- Other: first of three complementary GreAT project deliverables added this sweep (see also
  `great2021d22operationalArch`, `great2021d51avionicsArch`).
