## What it is

A follow-on to `honour2010seRoi`. It applies 14 PCA-derived program characterization parameters (7
quantitative, 7 subjective) to adjust SE effort, which sharply improves the correlation with success
(e.g., architecting vs. cost compliance from 13% to 62%). It offers an a-priori method to "right-size"
each of eight SE activities, with two worked example programs.

## Why it's valuable — and to what

- Literature review section: none of §2-§4.
- Decomposition / architecture (§6, §10): none directly; the architecting-effort result is a
  data point only.
- Stakeholder / objective ontology (§7-§9), optimization (§11-§13): not applicable.
- Glossary / terminology: same eight SE activities and success measures as `honour2010seRoi`.
- Other: the only content that goes beyond the 2010 paper is the method for adjusting the optimum SE
  level by program size/complexity. That is of use if the report ever needs to justify how much SE
  rigor a program this size warrants; otherwise redundant with `honour2010seRoi`.
