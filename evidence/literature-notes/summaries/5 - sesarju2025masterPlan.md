## What it is

An authoritative institutional planning document — the 2025 edition of the European ATM Master
Plan, the central planning tool for ATM modernisation under the EU's Single European Sky (SES)
initiative. Sets out the "Digital European Sky" vision (target: 2045), five technological
transformation levers (trajectory-based operations, data volumes, automation, dynamic airspace,
human operator role), a new service-oriented delivery model, a four-phase roll-out plan, 10
strategic deployment objectives (SDOs), 12 development priorities, and benefit/investment/risk
analysis (including ROI figures: EUR 17-53 per euro invested by 2050).
