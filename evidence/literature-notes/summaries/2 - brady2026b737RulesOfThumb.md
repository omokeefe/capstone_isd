## What it is

A one-page list of mental-arithmetic rules for 737 pilots: speed, height and distance conversions, approach planning gates, thrust and pitch settings, and fuel formulae. Two of its tables are cited to Boeing's Flight Planning and Performance Manual and cover the 737-300 and 737-700.

## Why it's valuable — and to what

- Optimization study (§11–§13): the only source found that states 737 pilot rules of thumb directly. Used throughout `knowledge/models/b737-max9-rules-of-thumb.md`.
- Nothing for §2–§10.

## What was taken from it (2026-10-10)

- "Level flight deceleration allow 10kts/nm & 1kt/sec (deceleration is faster at lower weights)"; "Descending deceleration allow 5kts/nm & 0.5kt/sec"; "Idle descent allow 3nm/1000'".
- "Aim for 250kts, 10,000ft by 30nm out"; "Aim for 210kts, On ILS at 12nm".
- Fuel penalty off optimum altitude, 737-700 at Mach 0.78: 2 percent at 2,000 ft above or below, 5 percent at 4,000 ft below, 14 percent at 8,000 ft below.
- "The 737 burns approx 30kg/min."; "Trip Fuel Reduction = Weight reduction x Flight time in hrs x 3.5%".

## Flags

- **Unofficial, and not MAX data.** No rule on the page is stated for the 737 MAX. Most do not name a variant; the thrust and pitch table is for a 47.5 (tonne) aircraft.
- **The Boeing manual it cites was not seen**, so the two tables are second-hand.
- **Read depth:** full page text extracted by script and read. The owner has not read it.
- The site sells a book with the same content; the page is a personal reference site, not a publisher.

## Rating

**2/5** — The most directly on-topic page found, but unofficial, for older variants, and second-hand for its tables. Good for orientation and for questions to put to the owner's own experience; weak as a citation.
