## What it is

The FAA's controlling document for air traffic control procedures and phraseology: it defines controller
positions and team responsibilities, clearances, separation, handoffs and coordination, and the
phraseology controllers use. Organized in chapters — 1 General, 2 General Control, 3 Airport Traffic
Control–Terminal, 4 IFR, 5 Radar, 6 Nonradar, 7 Visual, 8 Offshore/Oceanic, 9 Special Flights,
10 Emergencies, 11 Traffic Management Procedures, 12 Canadian Airspace, 13 Decision Support Tools —
plus Pilot/Controller Glossary. It governs ATC only; it does not cover flight-crew or airline dispatch
duties.
