## What it is

Presents the Engineering Systems Multiple-Domain Matrix (ES-MDM), a conceptual and
modeling framework for organizing information about large-scale, complex "engineering
systems" (systems with heavy social, technical, and organizational intricacy) — addressing
what information is needed to describe such a system, where it resides, how to elicit it,
and how to use it, building on Design Structure Matrix (DSM) methods.
