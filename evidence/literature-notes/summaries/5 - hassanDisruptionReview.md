## What it is

A critical review and classification of airline disruption-management literature
2009-2018, covering aircraft recovery, crew recovery, passenger recovery, and integrated
(multi-resource) recovery approaches, with a discussion of the gap between published
research and the reality of Airline Operations Control Centers (AOCCs).

## Why it's valuable — and to what

- Literature review section: `to-do-list.md` §4 ("Airline Operations Control /
  Disruption Management Literature") — directly the review this section is built on.
- Decomposition / architecture (§6, §10): distinguishes aircraft/crew/passenger recovery as
  separable-but-coupled decision domains, useful for OCC-related architecture elements.
- Stakeholder / objective ontology (§7-§9): recovery objectives/costs (delay cost, crew
  overtime, fuel, re-accommodation cost) are exactly the §8 airline cost categories.
- Optimization study (§11-§13): surveys exact/heuristic/hybrid optimization methods for
  recovery — a strong candidate methodology reference if the optimization case study touches
  disruption recovery.
- Glossary / terminology: AOCC, aircraft/crew/passenger recovery terminology.
