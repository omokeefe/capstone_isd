## What it is

A primary research paper (not a review) presenting integrated models and algorithms that
simultaneously optimize flight-leg selection (schedule design) and fleet-type assignment,
using real data from a major U.S. airline, in contrast to the traditional sequential
approach.

## Why it's valuable — and to what

- Literature review section: `to-do-list.md` §2 — a concrete example of "integrated
  optimization" that `eltoukhy2017airline` discusses at a survey level.
- Decomposition / architecture (§6, §10): demand/supply interaction concepts (unconstrained
  vs. constrained demand, spill, hub-and-spoke network structure) are useful vocabulary for
  the flight-operations domain's information objects.
- Stakeholder / objective ontology (§7-§9): explicit objective — maximize revenue, minimize
  operating cost, subject to capacity/seat constraints — is a clean instance of the §8
  airline objective category, and a concrete counterexample to the sequential/myopic
  approach for §9.
- Optimization study (§11-§13): a strong methodological template if the optimization case
  study lands on schedule design + fleet assignment specifically.
